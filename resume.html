Research

  • 2026–

    AegisLand — UAV perception experiments

    Independent · Python · simulation

    Built simulation experiments to test one question: when a landing camera is confidently wrong, can an independent estimate catch the error? On an early synthetic holdout, a selective supervisor reduced unsafe touchdowns from 43% to 1%. A later camera holdout failed several preregistered gates: 20% of truth-visible frames were missed, tail-error targets failed, and 95% uncertainty coverage fell to 84.3% lateral / 79.7% altitude under shift. I kept that failed result frozen instead of retuning on it.

  • 2026

    AbstainBench

    Independent · JavaScript · WebLLM

    Built a small local benchmark for a concrete failure mode: models answering when they should say they do not know. It records answer accuracy, hallucinations, and false abstentions across a fixed question set, with optional in-browser inference.
