# Space photography

These images provide context for Suhas's interest in aerospace. They are historical NASA imagery, not Suhas's research results or evidence of NASA affiliation.

- **earthrise.webp**: Apollo 8's *Earthrise*, photographed by Bill Anders, December 24, 1968. Credit: NASA / Bill Anders. [NASA source](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/apollo-8s-iconic-earthrise/). Source image AS08-14-2383, resized to 2400 × 2400 and encoded as WebP.
- **jupiter.webp**: Voyager 2 view of Jupiter's Great Red Spot, PIA01370. Credit: NASA / JPL. [NASA source](https://science.nasa.gov/image-detail/amf-pia01370/). Source composite retained at 800 × 920 and encoded as WebP.
