I.

AegisLand

Simulation only. Independent work on perception overconfidence and calibrated abstention for UAV safety.

Evidence · The exhibit

AegisLand is a simulation-only research cockpit for UAV perception reliability. Not flight software. Not a safety case. An inspectable archive where unsafe rates and abstention behavior stay visible.

This is the flagship work. The holdouts stay frozen, and the failures stay visible.

Method · How it is built

Simulation-first research means the test set is frozen before any tuning. No post-exposure adjustments. The cockpit displays what happened, including what failed.

Each phase locks a holdout set. Results are recorded. Retuning after seeing the holdout is not a result. It is overfitting with extra steps.
