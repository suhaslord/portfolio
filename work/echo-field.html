Enter the field GitHub

Why it exists

Research pages can make a person look like they only write tables. I also make. ECHO / FIELD is the proof: pointer motion changes the flow, microphone energy can change density, and the Memory channel seeds a new state. Begin a new field and the phrase, seed, and visual all shift in seconds.

Shipped with guided onboarding, a live inspector for palette / density / drift / seed, six-slot local field memory, PNG export, short WebM recording, and mobile controls. Fonts from Google when online; no third-party visual APIs at runtime.

This is craft adjacent to a SkillsUSA Regional Championship in 3D Visualization & Animation. Different medium, same intolerance for a dead image.
