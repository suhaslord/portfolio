A bit about me

Aerospace got me curious.
Building software gave me a way in.

I'm a high school student drawn to problems where code meets the physical world. I like taking an idea far enough to see what happens when its assumptions stop holding.

That has taken me from spacecraft dynamics to camera uncertainty and language-model evaluation. I've also worked on model routing, memory boundaries, and QA as an AI engineering intern at Seagulls / OpenStage.

I'm interested in internships and collaborations where I can contribute to a real codebase and learn from the people building it.
