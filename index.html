My résumé ↗

I'm Suhas, a high school student drawn to problems where code meets the physical world — and to software that helps people do something better.

Aerospace got me curious. Building software gave me a way to explore spacecraft dynamics, camera uncertainty, language-model evaluation, navigation, astronomy, and climate data.

At Seagulls / OpenStage, I worked as part of a crew on model routing, memory boundaries, guardrails, tracing, and QA. I’m careful about the difference between my implementation, a team result, and an interesting idea.

I'm interested in internships and collaborations where I can contribute to a real codebase and keep learning in public.
