I'm a student at River Islands High School, taking college CS at Delta College, and running an independent research program on when perception — cameras or language models — should refuse to act.

I care about questions that survive contact with a holdout: Does it work reliably? Can I measure it? What happens when the training distribution moves? If the answer is mixed, I freeze it. Retuning after exposure is not a result.
